Math for change amounts for companies:

Math.floor(Math.pow(Math.random(), exponent) * range) + 1
```

`Math.random()` gives you 0–1 uniform. Raising it to a power **skews the distribution**:

- `exponent = 1` — flat uniform, no bias
- `exponent > 1` — biased toward **low numbers** (most rolls land small, rare big hits)
- `exponent < 1` (e.g. 0.5) — biased toward **high numbers**
```

Tuesday March 17:
Established new updates to brand design.
Established 24 soundtrack list:

Themes: 
The Static Pole: Claustrophobia, suffocation, loneliness, hopelessness, mundainity, feeling trapped in stasis
The Kinetic Pole: equilibrium, flow of time, technology, corporate authority, sense of the world movign fast around you

How that maps to composition practically:

Harmonic language: Sustained minor modal harmony with no resolution — 
Dorian or Phrygian work well here. They have forward motion implied but 
never deliver it. Phrygian specifically has a corporate coldness to it, 
that flat second creates unease without being overtly dramatic.

Rhythm: Two simultaneous layers moving at different rates. 
A slow human pulse underneath — something felt around 40-50 BPM, 
your static pole. A mechanical layer on top running at a faster 
subdivision — your kinetic pole. The player lives in the gap between them. 
This is what Vangelis did architecturally in Bladerunner.

Melody: Sparse, incomplete phrases. A line that starts and doesn't finish, 
or finishes somewhere unexpected. Loneliness and hopelessness live in phrases 
that don't resolve where the ear expects. Never Forget works this way — the melody 
implies a cadence that keeps deferring.

Negative space: Your claustrophobia and suffocation themes are best served 
paradoxically by removing sound rather than adding density. A dense 
mix feels overwhelming but not claustrophobic. A sparse mix with one close, 
dry sound — a breath, a mechanical click, a single piano note — and nothing 
else creates the feeling of walls. The player can hear the room they're trapped in.

Corporate authority: This one lives in timbre, not melody. Cold, 
processed, synthetic textures that don't decay naturally. Everything 
that represents the system should have an unnaturally long sustain or an 
unnaturally fast cutoff — nothing organic. Underneath the human elements, 
the corporate layer just runs. Constant. Indifferent.


Your cue categories based on those themes:

Exploration / mundane existence — the ODST street equivalent. 
Slow felt tempo, incomplete melodic phrases, heavy negative space, 
the mechanical underlayer present but quiet. This is where the 
claustrophobia and flow of time live.

Corporate spaces — cold synthetic beds, no melody, just texture 
and that indifferent mechanical pulse. Authority expressed as 
something that doesn't acknowledge the player.

Moments of weight — loneliness and hopelessness peak here. 
Single instrument, very dry, very close. The Minecraft model 
applies — short, plays once, long silence after. The player feels 
it precisely because it doesn't stay.

Tension without combat — equilibrium disrupted but not broken. 
The static and kinetic poles moving out of sync with each other. 
Harmonic ambiguity increasing without resolving into action.

The through-line connecting all of it is that nothing in your soundtrack 
should feel like it was written for the player. It was already playing 
before they arrived. That's the mundanity and the corporate authority 
expressed musically — the world doesn't score itself around you.

(Exploration) > nocturnes
Lower Depths - Static Pole, claustrophobic, cramped, Phrgian Mode
The Corridors - Static Pole, less cramped, but still has overbearing weight.
Mega Hallway - Static and Kenetic Poles. Vast open space with reverb.
Mid Levels - More ordered world, more corporate, colder, more synthetic.
Approaching the Surface - Tension between hope and the weight of everything down bellow
The Surface - The experience of living in a less weighted world.

(Work and Economy) > Corporate authority has building passacaglias
Lower Work - Mechanical, static pole, menial, feeling trapped.
Mid Work - Slightly more melodic, has more cognitive weight. Cold.
Surface Work - Consequencial jobs, more orchestral weight. Resolution of the ecenomic arc.
Market/Economy Lower - Chaotic but contained. Question and answer melodies with transaction.
Market/Economy Mid - The individual themes are smaller. More synthetic, less human.
Corporate Authority

(Combat) Tocattas and Fugues. Look at Bach's Toccata and Fugue in D minor.
Lower Combat - Desperate survival, hopelessness
Mid Combat - More calculated. More tactical.
Surface Combat Consequences, high stake, most orchestral but filtered through player theme.
Crime/Stealth Low - Tension without percussion. Static and kenetic poles barely moving. Maximum ambiguity.
